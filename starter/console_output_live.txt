### Executive Summary

This validation roadmap outlines a 3-month strategy for evaluating the repurposing of three drug candidates—Roluperidone, Siramesine, and Pitolisant Hydrochloride—for the treatment of non-alcoholic steatohepatitis (NASH) through the modulation of TMEM97. The goal is to assess the efficacy and safety of these candidates in preclinical models and determine their potential for clinical application. Each month will focus on specific objectives, culminating in a go/no-go decision based on gathered data and analysis.

### Month 1: Target Engagement

**Objectives:**
- Assess the binding affinity and functional activity of each candidate on TMEM97.
- Evaluate the pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of the candidates in relevant biological systems.

**Activities:**
1. **In vitro Binding Studies:**
   - Conduct assays to measure the binding affinity of Roluperidone, Siramesine, and Pitolisant Hydrochloride to TMEM97.
   - Utilize surface plasmon resonance (SPR) or similar techniques.

2. **Functional Assays:**
   - Evaluate the impact of each drug on TMEM97-mediated pathways in cell lines expressing TMEM97.
   - Measure downstream effects on lipid metabolism and inflammation markers.

3. **Pharmacokinetic Profiling:**
   - Analyze the absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion (ADME) properties of each candidate in animal models.

**Data Limitations:**
- Potential variability in cell line responses.
- Limited availability of TMEM97-specific assays.

### Month 2: Disease Models

**Objectives:**
- Validate the efficacy of the candidates in preclinical models of NASH.
- Assess the safety profile and potential side effects.

**Activities:**
1. **Animal Models:**
   - Utilize established NASH models (e.g., high-fat diet-induced models) to evaluate the therapeutic effects of each candidate.
   - Monitor liver histology, lipid accumulation, and inflammatory markers.

2. **Dosing Regimen:**
   - Determine optimal dosing schedules based on pharmacokinetic data from Month 1.
   - Implement both short-term and long-term treatment protocols.

3. **Safety Assessments:**
   - Conduct toxicity studies to evaluate liver function and overall health of the animals.
   - Monitor for adverse effects and dose-limiting toxicities.

**Data Limitations:**
- Differences in metabolism between animal models and humans.
- Limited understanding of long-term effects in chronic dosing scenarios.

### Month 3: Go/No-Go Decision

**Objectives:**
- Analyze data from target engagement and disease models to make an informed decision on the candidates.
- Prepare a comprehensive report summarizing findings and recommendations.

**Activities:**
1. **Data Analysis:**
   - Compile and analyze data from binding studies, functional assays, and animal model outcomes.
   - Compare efficacy and safety profiles of Roluperidone, Siramesine, and Pitolisant Hydrochloride.

2. **Decision Matrix:**
   - Develop a decision matrix based on efficacy, safety, and feasibility of clinical translation.
   - Engage stakeholders for input on the decision-making process.

3. **Final Report:**
   - Prepare a detailed report summarizing findings, including recommendations for further development or discontinuation of candidates.
   - Present findings to stakeholders for final approval.

**Data Limitations:**
- Potential biases in data interpretation.
- Limited sample sizes in preclinical studies may affect generalizability.

### Conclusion

This roadmap provides a structured approach to validate the repurposing of Roluperidone, Siramesine, and Pitolisant Hydrochloride for NASH treatment via TMEM97 modulation. By focusing on target engagement, disease modeling, and a final decision-making process, we aim to ensure a thorough evaluation of these candidates within a 3-month timeframe.
